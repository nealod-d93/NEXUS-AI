🧪 DDPM training checklist

A practical dataset workflow for later compatible training:

  1. Collect images you have permission to use.
  2. Resize/crop consistently and remove corrupted files.
  3. Split into train and validation sets.
  4. Choose a small diffusion architecture and verify RAM/storage.
  5. Train, save checkpoints, and compare generated samples.

⚙️ Device diagnostics

Running checks…

Local inference requires WebGPU, enough free RAM, and a supported browser. Model downloads require internet; once cached, offline use may be possible depending on browser storage.

If installation isn't offered, open the browser menu and choose “Add to Home screen”.

Privacy & storage

Chat runs through the selected in-browser model after it loads. Model files can use substantial storage. Clearing site data may remove cached models and saved prompts. Do not upload private files to websites you do not trust.
